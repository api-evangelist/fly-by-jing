The Rise of the “Seed Oils Are Bad” Narrative In recent years, seed oils have been vilified in certain diet and social media circles, despite longstanding use in cuisines worldwide. Since around 2018, a wave of online misinformation has targeted vegetable oils extracted from seeds – e.g. canola, corn, sunflower, soybean, cottonseed, safflower, grapeseed, rice bran – blaming them for nearly every “disease of affluence”.
