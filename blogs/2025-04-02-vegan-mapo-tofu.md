Mapo tofu (麻婆豆腐, mápó dòufu) is a popular dish from Sichuan, China, a province in southwest China known for its hot and spicy dishes. It’s a meal that is rich, creamy, spicy, numbing, and umami all at once. A bowl of mapo tofu over steaming hot rice is the ultimate comfort food.
