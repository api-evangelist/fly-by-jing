There are many easy and delicious vegan options in Chinese cuisine, and you can begin recreating stir-fries, noodle dishes , dumplings, and a whole lot more in your own kitchen today. Sweet and sour tofu is an adaptation of a classic Chinese dish that the whole family is sure to love, and you can change out your favorite vegetable ingredients and spices to find your favorite heat levels and texture. One of the most important elements in Chinese cooking is the sauces, especially in vegan cooking.
