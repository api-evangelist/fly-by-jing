Rapeseed oil has many health and flavor benefits, and it’s an excellent addition to any home cook’s pantry, particularly if you’re interested in making Chinese dishes. It is part of the cabbage family and can have traces of flavors like turnip or mustard but without overpowering fragrances or tastes. Rapeseed oil can be associated with canola oil, and while there are some similarities, there are key differences as well.
