Vegan eating is growing in popularity, thanks to its many health benefits, the reduced impact a vegan diet has on the environment, and the growth of vegan companies and alternatives that make it easier to try out vegan cooking at home and when dining out. When you think about vegan dining, Chinese food might not immediately come to mind, but the truth is that meat alternatives and vegan cooking options have been part of Chinese culture for a long time, and you can embrace classic vegan cooking and modern ingredients alike in your own kitchen today. Fly By Jing is here to help.
