If you’ve ever eaten at a Chinese food restaurant or enjoyed Chinese food take-out in the United States, chances are that you’ve come across the ingredient MSG (monosodium glutamate). Of all the ingredients that make up Chinese cooking and the American Chinese cuisine experience, none are more representative than MSG. It is not merely an important ingredient for adding and boosting your favorite Chinese food flavors.
