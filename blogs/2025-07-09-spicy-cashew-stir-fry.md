Chinese food dishes are a blend of sweet, savory, and umami flavors, which means that nuts are an excellent addition to many meals. They add a hint of nutty, savory flavor that helps to make your favorite Chinese food dishes really stand out, and can be adjusted for heat level, flavor profile, and ingredients, so you can find—and make—the recipes you really love. This spicy cashew stir fry dish is the perfect place to start, and Fly By Jing has the ingredients you’ll want for your pantry when you’re ready to begin making Chinese food from the comfort of home.
