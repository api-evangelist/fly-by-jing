Soy sauce is one of those Chinese food cooking essentials that give so much savory and umami flavor to your favorite Chinese food dishes that they just wouldn’t be the same without it. But for all its ubiquity and versatility, soy sauce is a more complex and historical condiment that has many unique variations and uses. The more you know about the nuances between soy sauce flavors and how each one can be used, the easier it will be to achieve those amazing Chinese food flavors at home.
