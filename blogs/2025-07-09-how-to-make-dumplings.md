NOTE FROM JING: My identity is wrapped around Zhong dumplings, one of the most famous street snacks in Chengdu. I’ve been obsessed with them since childhood, and I still picture my first bite at a fly restaurant near my grandparents’ house, the chili oil dribbling down my chin and my eyes wide as I tasted the indescribably delicious combination of juicy pork, sweet soy sauce, garlic, and sesame. I was instantly hooked, and years later, this was the core memory that brought me back to Chengdu to begin my journey of building Fly By Jing.
