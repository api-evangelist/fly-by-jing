Chinese food is an ever-evolving cuisine that takes influence from all the different regions of China and changes with each new generation. There are also many different Chinese dishes that have come out of the immigrant experience around the world, as well, with immigration into China providing new flavors and recipes and emigration around the world providing access to new ingredients. Here at Fly By Jing, we want to make it easy to add your own personal touch to Chinese food dishes from history.
