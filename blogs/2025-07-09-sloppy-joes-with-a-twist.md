When it comes to making your favorite Chinese food dishes at home or integrating hot, sweet, or umami Chinese flavors into western foods, there’s nothing bolder or more versatile than chili sauce. Chili sauce can be used as a condiment, mixed in during the cooking process, or drizzled over your favorite dishes for a little extra kick right at the end. Chinese chili sauce is balanced in texture and flavor and can be adjusted to match your heat level.
